ParentSquare · AI thesis · October 2026

In the age of AI, trusted human networks grow in value.

AI makes information and digital interactions abundant. The scarce thing is trusted connection to real people—and opportunities to gather in the real world.
The future is not more time with an assistant. It is more human participation, made easier by AI.
01 · The strategic asset

ParentSquare already connects real communities.

Schools are trusted institutions. ParentSquare connects educators, families and organizers around a shared place and a shared purpose.
From communication → coordination → participation.
02 · The activation opportunity

The network is installed. Participation is not automatic.

Community Groups supports PTOs, volunteers and school communities. But a new organizer may not know it exists—or how to get it enabled.
The growth opportunity is activating latent community demand inside an existing customer relationship.
03 · Public evidence

There is already a path to the first group.

Circle Public Schools documents a school-controlled PTO group-request process. Watertown publishes Community Group signup pages.
These prove the workflow exists; they do not establish the size of the inactive-school cohort.
No publicly verified zero-group school has been identified. Confirm this cohort with ParentSquare internal data.
04 · Imagine the discovery moment

“I want to bring parents together.”

An organizer asks an AI assistant how to run school events and recruit volunteers. Instead of recommending a new platform, the assistant recognizes the existing school relationship.
I'm organizing our school's first family event. How can I recruit parents and coordinate volunteers?
Which school? I can check whether you already have a trusted school-community platform.

05 · The growth loop

Discovery creates real-world participation.

A parent discovers an existing capability, launches a group, invites families, and organizes the first event. Repeat participation embeds ParentSquare more deeply in school life.

06 · The test

Prove activation before claiming retention.

Identify eligible schools with no active Community Groups. Test assisted onboarding against the normal path. Track group activation, members, first event and 30/90-day activity.
Leading indicators
Activation

Approved groups, first posts/events, participating families, organizer repeat use.

Later validation
Business impact

Broader adoption, account engagement and retention—tested rather than assumed.

North-star: newly active groups per 100 eligible previously inactive schools.
07 · The expansion

First activate. Then displace.

Once ParentSquare is the natural home for new groups, tackle established off-platform workflows. Trabuco Mesa PTA uses SignUpGenius, but why remains unverified.
First wedge
Inactive school → first group

No existing competing workflow to uproot.

Second wedge
External workflow → ParentSquare

Earn the switch by delivering better community outcomes.

Long-term opportunity: ParentSquare becomes the place where school communities move from messages to meeting.
