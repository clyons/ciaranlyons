Price is not simply the number on the receipt. Information can grow cheaper to distribute while becoming more valuable in the right place; abundance makes other things scarce; and a purchase still demands time and effort before its value is realized.

The things that act as currency are broader than money, too: attention, status, access, trust, and solutions to real problems. These quotes are a reminder to ask not only what something costs, but what kind of value it makes possible—and what it asks in return.
